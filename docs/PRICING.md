# Seed pricing note

Seed prices are editable development values, not permanent market claims. They were calibrated on 24 August 2026 against current Indian online listings and observed bands:

- Flipkart plant listings showed examples including Golden Money Plant ₹244, Peace Lily ₹170–₹227, ZZ Plant ₹264 and Red Anthurium ₹799.
- IndNursery showed common small plants around ₹199–₹429, including Pink Syngonium ₹207 and potted Snake Plant ₹249–₹429.
- Paudhewale described popular indoor plants commonly around ₹199–₹699 with a basic pot.

Sources:

- https://www.flipkart.com/q/indoor-plants
- https://indnursery.com/
- https://www.paudhewale.com/browse/plants

Sivorment models base, sale, variant, city, seller and promotional prices separately so operators can update prices without editing frontend markup.
